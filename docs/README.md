# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

OctoAcme manages cross-functional projects through a customer-first, iterative lifecycle with clear ownership and data-informed decisions. Initiation validates the need, aligns stakeholders, and defines success measures. Planning turns approved work into a prioritized backlog with acceptance criteria, owners, milestones, and risk and dependency plans. During execution, teams deliver in small increments, track progress on a project board, review and test changes, and communicate status and blockers. Releases follow readiness checks, deployment verification, and stakeholder communication. Retrospectives turn lessons learned into tracked improvement actions.

Project Managers coordinate delivery, schedules, risks, and communication; Product Managers define outcomes and priorities; developers, QA, and stakeholders contribute to implementation, validation, and decisions. Teams share regular updates, review risks, and escalate blockers as needed.
