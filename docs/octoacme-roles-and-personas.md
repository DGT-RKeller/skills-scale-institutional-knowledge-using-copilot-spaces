# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Executive Sponsors / Business Owners

### Role Summary
Executive Sponsors or Business Owners connect project work to organizational strategy. They provide direction on business outcomes and help resolve issues that exceed the project team's authority.

### Responsibilities
- Confirm strategic objectives and expected business outcomes
- Secure or approve funding and major resource commitments
- Make or escalate major scope and priority decisions
- Remove organizational blockers outside the team's control

### Goals
- Ensure the project supports strategic priorities
- Enable timely decisions on significant trade-offs
- Realize the intended business outcomes

### Typical Communication
- Project kickoff and major decision reviews
- Executive status updates and escalations
- Business outcome and funding reviews

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Align with the Product Manager on business outcomes, product priorities, and major trade-offs
- Work with the Project Manager on escalations, project health, and decisions affecting scope, funding, or timing
- Provide direction to the team through the Product Manager and Project Manager while leaving day-to-day delivery decisions to the team

---

## Engineering Leads / Technical Leads

### Role Summary
Engineering Leads or Technical Leads guide technical implementation and help the team deliver a maintainable solution. They coordinate engineering decisions, estimates, and technical dependencies.

### Responsibilities
- Guide technical design and implementation decisions
- Help Developers estimate, sequence, and coordinate engineering work
- Identify technical risks, dependencies, and constraints
- Support code quality and consistency across the engineering effort

### Goals
- Deliver a secure, reliable, and maintainable technical solution
- Make technical risks and trade-offs visible early
- Help the team make feasible delivery commitments

### Typical Communication
- Technical design discussions and decision records
- Engineering planning and dependency reviews
- Code reviews and technical risk updates

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Work with Developers on designs, implementation, reviews, and technical problem-solving
- Partner with the Product Manager to assess feasibility and explain technical trade-offs that affect product scope or outcomes
- Partner with the Project Manager on estimates, dependencies, and technical risks that affect plans or milestones

---

## Quality Assurance / Test Leads

### Role Summary
Quality Assurance or Test Leads coordinate the approach to validating that a project meets its quality goals and acceptance criteria. They surface quality risks and readiness concerns before release.

### Responsibilities
- Define test and validation approaches for project deliverables
- Coordinate test coverage, execution, and defect triage
- Track quality risks and communicate unresolved issues
- Assess and report whether release quality criteria have been met

### Goals
- Detect defects and quality risks early
- Provide clear evidence of whether deliverables meet acceptance criteria
- Support predictable, informed release decisions

### Typical Communication
- Test plans, results, and defect reports
- Quality reviews and release-readiness discussions
- Coordination with Developers during validation and triage

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Coordinate with Developers to plan testing, reproduce and resolve defects, and improve quality
- Align with the Product Manager on acceptance criteria and with the Project Manager on quality milestones and risks
- Share validation results and readiness concerns with the Operations / Release Owner before deployment

---

## Operations / Release Owners

### Role Summary
Operations or Release Owners coordinate deployment and operational readiness. They help ensure that a change can be released, supported, and monitored safely.

### Responsibilities
- Plan deployment and operational readiness activities
- Coordinate release execution, including dependencies and rollback plans
- Confirm monitoring, support, and operational procedures are ready
- Monitor service health after release and coordinate incident response when needed

### Goals
- Release changes safely and predictably
- Minimize operational disruption and recovery time
- Ensure the delivered service can be supported after launch

### Typical Communication
- Release plans and readiness checklists
- Deployment coordination and operational handoffs
- Service health and post-release updates

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Coordinate with Developers on deployment needs, technical dependencies, and rollback plans
- Review validation results and release criteria with the Quality Assurance / Test Lead
- Work with the Project Manager on release timing, dependencies, and stakeholder communications
- Notify the Product Manager of release outcomes or operational issues that affect users or expected outcomes

---

## Customer / Stakeholder Representatives

### Role Summary
Customer or Stakeholder Representatives communicate user and stakeholder needs and provide feedback on proposed or delivered work. They help the team validate assumptions and outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Share user needs, operational context, and stakeholder constraints
- Provide timely feedback on requirements, prototypes, and deliverables
- Participate in reviews or validation against agreed acceptance criteria
- Communicate emerging needs or concerns to the project team

### Goals
- Ensure project work addresses real user and stakeholder needs
- Make feedback and decisions available early enough to guide delivery
- Support adoption of useful project outcomes

### Typical Communication
- Requirements and discovery sessions
- Demonstrations, reviews, and acceptance discussions
- Feedback channels and stakeholder updates

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Work with the Product Manager to clarify needs, validate priorities, and assess whether outcomes meet user expectations
- Coordinate with the Project Manager on review timing, decisions, and feedback dependencies
- Provide Developers and the Quality Assurance / Test Lead with relevant context and feedback through the agreed product and project processes

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- Role titles and ownership may vary by team; smaller teams may combine responsibilities while keeping decision ownership clear.
